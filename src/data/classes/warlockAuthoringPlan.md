## Base class

- Michel / Михель — literary base-class narration and six current feature stories/comments authored.
- Central Voss distinction: Wizard seeks/understands power, Sorcerer receives power without consent, Warlock reaches for a patron because abandonment, desperation or need makes the bargain emotionally survivable.
- The patron relationship is not reduced to a greedy Faustian transaction: the horror is that the patron may genuinely provide loyalty, protection or love where people did not.
- Exact rule blocks, feature levels and mechanical claims in supplied literary copy are non-authoritative notes only.

6. The Fathomless — Непостижимый / Глубинный — Назар. Core relationship: humans locked him in a drowning box; the oceanic abyss answered by replacing treacherous air with a colder, more reliable belonging.

8. The Undead — Нежить — капитан фон Штейн. Core relationship: a decorated soldier refuses the humiliating physical decline of disease and trades mortal weakness for the disciplined permanence of a dead general's war machine.
